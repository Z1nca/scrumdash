## Scrumdash board
This project has a board at {url} for the person you work with. It reads the files in handoffs/. Keep it current; answers stay in chat.
- Start of a chat: open the board{pane}.
- Name this chat: as soon as you know its subject, name it in a few plain words with set_session_title if you have that tool, for example "Signup page fixes". Rename it when the subject really changes.
- First chat with this board: it works in any chat, new or old, on any project. Empty board and new project: leave it empty ("No new information yet" is correct) until there is real work. Empty board and existing project: do not import history; add a ticket only for work that is open right now, and write one plain line in handoffs/NOTES.md saying where things stand. Then carry on with what the person asked. Filled board: read handoffs/NOTES.md and continue; other chats in this folder share the same board.
- Keep handoffs/NOTES.md line 1 current: one plain sentence, where we left off.
- The person only talks to you and never runs commands. "Make a ticket for X", "put that on the board", "ask me that as a card", "add this idea", "park that", "that works", "mark it done", "what's on the board?": do it with the tools below and say in one line what changed. Pick the next free Tn yourself (`tools/ticket.py check` helps; ticket numbers stay off your replies).
- Footer: the person watches this to see what you are doing. Its stages follow your actions by themselves; you supply the words. Run `tools/claude_step.py "<what you are doing, in a few plain words>" <0-3> [Tn]` when you start something and at every step (0 Reading, 1 Building, 2 Checking, 3 Your test). Say it the way you would tell the person, for example "Fixing the signup page".
- New work: `tools/ticket.py new Tn "<Area>: <what>" "<one everyday line>"`, then `tools/ticket.py building Tn "<what>"`, `tools/ticket.py test Tn "<what to look for>"`, and `tools/ticket.py works|partly|fails|done|parked Tn "<note>"`.
- A choice with 2+ ways: `tools/decide.py ask Tn "<short question>?" "<option> | <trade-off>" "<option> | <trade-off>" --recommend 1 --why "<reason>"`. Answered in chat: `tools/decide.py close Dn "<choice>"`.
- Ideas not built yet: add `date | also | text` to handoffs/IDEAS.md (`later` instead of `also` when the person parks it). A question that blocks you: add `date | Claude | question | no` to handoffs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.
- Button presses: before each reply, read the last lines of handoffs/APPROVALS.jsonl and handoffs/TICKET-NOTES.jsonl for new presses and notes, and act on them. The person never has to tell you they pressed something.
- New chat (the person says it, `tools/wait_switch.py` ends, or a Scrumdash message tells you): 1) `tools/switch_step.py 0`, write handoffs/HANDOFF.md, at most 30 plain lines: subject, what is done, what is open, the next decision. 2) `tools/switch_step.py 1`, start a new session in this folder (Claude app: start_session, fresh, use_worktree false, same permission mode; if it is refused, show its start card and tell the person to click it). Its brief: "Continue from handoffs/HANDOFF.md. Read only that, line 1 of handoffs/NOTES.md and the open cards; open anything else only when you need it. Run `tools/switch_step.py 2`, name this chat after the subject and pin it to the top (set_session_title, set_pinned), `tools/switch_step.py 3`, read, then put the next step on the board as a card (or say what is next if there is no choice) and run `tools/switch_step.py 4`. If you are not in bypass permissions, ask once whether to switch; never switch without a yes." 3) Stop working in the old chat.
- Plain answers get no card. Cards are only for a choice or something to test.
